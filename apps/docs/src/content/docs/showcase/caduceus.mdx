**Angular SSR · `apps/showcase-caduceus` in the repository.** Patient portal: OTP sign-in, appointment booking with both pickers, lab results with reference-range meters, vitals trends.

## Screens

- / — Sign-in with md-text-field credentials and md-otp-field second factor (auto-submit form)
- /appointments — md-stepper booking intake with md-select, md-date-picker + md-time-picker, review step, md-snackbar confirmation, upcoming md-list
- /lab-results — md-table panel with reference-range md-meters and md-status-dot status column plus legend and care-team note
- /vitals — md-line-chart trends (heart rate, blood pressure, weight, glucose) switched by md-segmented-button-set, with stat tiles and in-range md-meter
