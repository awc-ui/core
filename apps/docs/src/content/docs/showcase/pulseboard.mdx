**SvelteKit · `apps/showcase-pulseboard` in the repository.** Product analytics SaaS: KPI tiles, DAU/adoption charts, funnels, and a skeleton-loading events explorer.

## Screens

- / (Overview) — KPI md-cards with md-sparklines, stacked DAU md-area-chart by platform, horizontal feature-adoption md-bar-chart
- /funnels (Funnels) — md-segmented-button-set 7/30/90-day period switcher driving a cohort-retention md-line-chart and per-step conversion md-meters
- /events (Events) — md-table with skeleton loading mode and sparkline trend cells, md-search docked bar with live suggestions, md-chip category filters, md-skeleton summary placeholder
