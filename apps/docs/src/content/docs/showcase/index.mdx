Explore AWC UI through the business workflows you are building. Each showcase
combines Core components into a complete application, with examples for both
customer-facing services and staff workspaces.

Choose an industry below, then open an application for its demo, supported
frameworks, component breakdown, and suggested workflows to try. The showcases
use fictional business data; transactions, care records, and equipment controls
are demonstrations.

## Banking & finance

### [Credit risk analysis — Aurelia Bank](/showcase/credit-risk/)

A Credit Risk Console for bank risk analysts reviewing portfolio exposure,
sectors, counterparties, facilities, and covenants. Explore a watchlist and
stress-testing scenarios through dense tables, charts, and connected drill-downs.

### [Wealth management — Kestrel Private Bank](/showcase/wealth/)

An advisory workspace for private bankers managing households, portfolios, and
investment goals. Build rebalancing proposals, inspect positions, and explore an
order ticket with multi-step validation.

### [Retail banking & investing — Vela](/showcase/banking/)

A customer banking app for reviewing accounts, filtering statements, managing
cards, exchanging currencies, and exploring investments. Its responsive screens
demonstrate everyday banking workflows on phones and desktops, using fictional
accounts and simulated transactions.

## Healthcare & pharmaceuticals

### [Hospital case management — Medflow](/showcase/medflow/)

A workspace for clinicians and care coordinators to search patient worklists,
assign clinicians, track case progression, and inspect patient-flow charts.

### [Laboratory testing & review — Vela](/showcase/pharma/)

A pharmaceutical laboratory workspace for analysts and reviewers handling
compounds, samples, test assignments, and review queues. Follow a test run,
approve a review, or request a retest using fictional laboratory records.

## Travel & transportation

### [Accommodation discovery & booking — Roam](/showcase/booking/)

A travel booking app for guests searching destinations, comparing stays, saving
properties, and choosing dates and guest counts. Complete a demo checkout and
manage the reservation in My trips. Prices and availability are simulated.

### [Flight search & booking — Aero](/showcase/aero/)

An airline booking interface for travelers comparing one-way and return flights,
choosing fares, adding passenger details, and selecting seats and bags. Review
and manage demo trips with simulated availability and prices.

### [Metro station operations — Metro pulse](/showcase/metro-monitor/)

An operations workspace for network operators monitoring passenger flow,
filtering stations, and acknowledging or resolving incidents. Explore train
movement across 15 fictional stations on three lines. Telemetry, trains, and
incidents belong to the browser simulation.

## Media & entertainment

### [Video discovery & creator channels — Frame](/showcase/frame/)

A video platform for viewers browsing feeds, searching videos, following creator
channels, and organizing a personal library. Watch demo footage or import a local
video with working playback controls. Creator and engagement data are fictional.

### [Music libraries & studio editing — Cygnus](/showcase/music/)

A music app combining listener libraries, albums, and artist pages with an
arrangement timeline and mixer for studio workflows. Its shared transport keeps
playback state across navigation, but the playhead is simulated: the showcase
contains no audio or recordings.

### [Live events & ticket booking — Encore](/showcase/encore/)

A ticketing app for audiences discovering shows, choosing performances and ticket
areas, and completing a booking. Try discounts, demo checkout, downloadable
tickets, and cancellations. Shows and bookings are fictional.

## Social networks

### [Photo sharing & discovery — Lyra](/showcase/social/)

A social photo app for browsing a followed feed, exploring images, composing
posts, and visiting profiles. Notifications, captions, and comments demonstrate
content-led interfaces with generated artwork and fictional people and accounts.

### [Community feeds, groups & events — Corvus](/showcase/community/)

A social network for connecting with friends, joining groups, exploring events,
and taking part in threaded conversations. Explore reactions, membership states,
and nested posts with fictional people, groups, and events.

## Design & creative work

### [Visual design & image editing — Pictor](/showcase/design/)

A design studio for creating compositions from templates, assets, layers, and
live AWC components. Designers can adjust geometry and colors, undo edits, and
export SVG, PNG, or JSON. Work is kept in the browser; project and shared-file
metadata are demonstration data.

## Industry & utilities

### [Water-treatment plant monitoring — Sentinel](/showcase/scada/)

A SCADA-style workspace for plant operators inspecting assets, acknowledging
alarms, adding notes, and comparing flow, pressure, and power trends. Plant data and equipment controls are simulated.

## Smart homes

### [Home automation & energy monitoring — Habitat's Maple House](/showcase/smart-home/)

A home-control app for residents managing rooms, lights, blinds, climate, scenes,
and routine previews. Inspect energy readings and device states across the demo
residence. Device controls are simulated.
