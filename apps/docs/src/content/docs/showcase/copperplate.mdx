**Plain HTML (no build step) · `apps/showcase-copperplate` in the repository.** Touch-first restaurant POS: register, tender dialog, end-of-day report — 48px targets throughout.

## Screens

- Register — menu md-card grid with md-button-group modifiers and md-number-field quantities, plus running order md-list with md-badge counts, remove md-icon-buttons, and live totals
- Tender — md-dialog with md-segmented-button-set payment types (Card / Cash / Gift card) and completion md-snackbar
- End of day — KPI cards, sales md-table with sticky header and totals footer inside md-table-container, and md-pie-chart donut of sales mix by category
